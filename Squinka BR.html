😺 Squinka Royale

A huge top-down battle royale. Explore, find loot, hide behind cover, collect rare Spirit Shards, defeat bosses, and become the last Squinka standing.

🌲 Huge world

Forests, rocks, buildings, roads, rare abandoned cars, loot, and hidden places.

🤖 150 armed bots

They loot, fight, heal, use cover, hear gunshots, search, and some use their own Spirit upgrades.

✨ 100 Spirits

Your Spirit Book saves your collection between matches. If eliminated, you lose Spirits collected during that match and the Spirit you have equipped; your other saved Spirits stay safe.

👑 Bosses

Bosses have more health and can reward rare shards or Spirits.

Tip: this is a self-contained browser game prototype. Graphics are drawn in code, so no extra downloads or libraries are needed.

📖 Spirit Book

You can hold at most 3 Spirit Shards. Defeated bots may drop their Spirit; spend 1 shard nearby and press E to extract it. Collect and equip Spirits during a match. If eliminated, Spirits collected in that match and your equipped Spirit are lost. Other Spirits in your collection stay saved.
