Squinka Laws

In Squinka Laws you can see all the rules of the Squinka Kingdom, where squinkas are found




Law 1

The first Squinka law is that you can't take another Squinka's belongings unless you have permission.


Law 2

The second Squinka law is that if you find something which does not belong to you, you have to give it to the Squinka king so he can give it to the Squinka it belongs to. If the Squinka says he/she does not want what you found anymore you can keep it.


Law 3

The third Squinka law is that if one Squinka murders another Squinka, the other Squinka can got to jail for 45+ years or a death sentence depending on what happened.


Law 4

The forth Squinka law is that you have to show kidness when possible. It is ok if you don't show kindness, but it is not ok if you don't because you can get punished.


Law 5

The fifth Squinka law is that you can't grief anything if it's not yours.


Law 6

The sixth Squinka law is that you can't get into someone's property without there permission even if you are apart of the government unless it's very serious.


Law 7

The seventh Squinka law is that nudity in public is not allowed.


Law 8

The eighth Squinka law is that if you own a slave you have to have life in jail.


Law 9

You need a permit if you want to something like owning a gun, mining in the Squinka Kingdom's mountain mine, or driving/flying a new vehicle.
